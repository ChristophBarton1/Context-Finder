How it works

From "it's broken" to fixed — in three clicks.

No DevTools, no copy-pasting stack traces, no explaining. The extension does the technical part; you just click send.

1

It watches, locally

Console errors, crashes, failed requests — captured automatically on every page, even inside the preview iframes where AI-built apps actually run. The badge shows what it caught.

2

One click when it breaks

Open the popup, optionally click the broken element or add a screenshot, and hit send. Obvious secrets are redacted before anything leaves the popup.

3

The report lands in your chat

Straight into your Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0 or Base44 conversation — or prefilled in ChatGPT. Your AI sees exactly what broke, and the fix works on the first try.
